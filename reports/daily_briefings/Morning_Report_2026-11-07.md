### 1. [SwarmEvolve: Latent Agent Swarms for Evolutionary Discovery and Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483v1)
> ⚡ LLM 引导的进化搜索利用人工智能代理，通过迭代优化解决方案和组合高分方法来改进数学和计算问题的程序。然而，该过程
👤 Qile Jiang, George Em Karniadakis | 📅 2026-11-05

**详情:** LLM-guided evolutionary search uses AI agents to improve programs for mathematical and computational problems by iteratively refining solutions and combining high-scoring methods. However, the process is often slow because the LLM generates a long textual chain of thought for each round and must repeat this generation many times to maintain population diversity. We present SwarmEvolve, a framework in which hundreds of low-cost agents per round work in swarms, reason in latent space without decoding to text, and share information by passing KV caches. Each swarm studies the problem from a different angle. A novelty filter keeps the most distinct agent ideas by directly measuring the distances between the agents' hidden state vectors. Crossover of ideas happens naturally in latent space, as a summarizer agent receives the combined KV caches of all swarm members. On 21 problems in mathematical discovery and scientific computing, SwarmEvolve gets the highest average score and needs a median of only 3 to 8 minutes to reach the best scores that other state-of-the-art frameworks reach in 60 minutes. On several problems, SwarmEvolve with Qwen3 8B improves on the solutions that AlphaEvolve first found with frontier LLMs. Our work shows that agent swarms that communicate in latent space offer a fast, low-cost approach to program search for scientific problems.

**详情:** 机器学习代码应在研究人员选择的环境中运行，而非其最初编写的环境。当前，机器学习代码主要存在于两个生态系统中：NVIDIA GPU上的PyTorch和Google TPU上的JAX。在它们之间迁移代码库意味着需要在不同的框架和硬件上进行构建和测试。这种迁移（1）是一种实际需求，因为它为研究人员解锁了不同的硬件；（2）也是一个自然的基准测试：它涉及不同的框架和硬件，覆盖整个代码库，并且是长期且具有挑战性的。我们提出了XStack，一个用于评估代理在两个生态系统之间迁移机器学习代码库能力的基准测试。每个代理接收一个PyTorch代码库和一个干净的TPU机器，并必须交付一个JAX代码库。如果其输出满足我们基于源代码库已发布结果的评分标准，并且经过我们的认证和校准，则视为通过。代理的迁移仍然不可靠：Claude Opus 5通过率为75%，GPT-5.6-sol通过率为45%，其他所有代理的通过率均不超过40%。XStack是使机器学习代码摆脱其生态系统束缚的一步。我们发布XStack，旨在使跨框架和硬件的可靠迁移进展可衡量。

### 4. [Same Behavior, Different Mechanism: What Finite Experiments Can Certify About Integrated Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08469v1)
> ⚡ 可解释性、人工智能审计以及对机器意识的评估，越来越依赖于机制层面的量：系统因果转换定律的泛函，这些泛函描述了系统如何进行计算。
👤 Linqian Wu, Diaoxuan He | 📅 2026-11-05

**详情:** Interpretability, AI auditing and assessments of machine consciousness increasingly rest on mechanism-level quantities: functionals of a system's causal transition law that describe how it computes, not what it outputs. Experiments see that law only through finite, partial and noisy interventions. We ask when such a quantity is identifiable from an experiment's access, continuous in the mechanism and certifiable from finite data, and answer exactly for a deliberately demanding case - the integrated information $\varphi$ of integrated information theory - in stochastic networks of four and five units with known ground truth. Behavior can be silent: a recurrent and a feed-forward network with identical input-output laws at their response delay carry 0.28 and 0 bits, yet internal recordings separate them within 4-53 trials. Partial access can be just as blind: in our examples, units left out of a one-step recording impose precision floors of 0.30-1.02 bits that no sample size lowers, and in all-but-self parity networks, recordings of any length see several hidden units only through the product of their fidelities - a symmetry that one intervention broke in exact checks. The measure has sharp edges: because $\varphi$ selects a partition, and the cause and effect it specifies, by one criterion and reports another, infinitesimal changes of mechanism that cross an edge change it by 25-100% at 99% of the edges we found in random networks, and about 1% of surrogate models that fit interventional data almost perfectly misreport it by more than 20%. One-sided claims survive: the same data certify a lower bound on both sides of an edge, and we prove $\varphi&gt;1.004$ bits throughout a 64-dimensional region of mechanisms and certify it from about $10^7$ controlled transitions with false-certificate probability at most $α$. Measurability is thus a design question with computable answers.

### 1. [Rejoice, leap seconds are dead!](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2026/rejoice-the-utc-leap-second-is-dead/)
📍 jeffgeerling.com | 📅 Fri, 30 Oct 2026

**详情:** ## 技术情报分析报告：UTC 闰秒终结及其影响

**背景**

国际度量衡大会（CGPM）在2026年通过了第5号决议，正式宣布自2027年5月20日起，协调世界时（UTC）将不再引入或移除闰秒。这一决定标志着持续数十年的闰秒争议画上了句号，旨在解决闰秒给全球时间同步系统带来的复杂性和潜在风险。

**关键发现**

此次决议的核心在于，UTC将与地球自转时间（UT1）产生分离，允许其最多偏离1小时。这一改变的直接动因是地球自转速度近期呈现加速趋势，增加了引入“负闰秒”（即移除一秒）的可能性，而负闰秒的实现对现有计算机系统而言将是前所未有的挑战，可能引发比Y2K更严重的混乱。工业界和关键基础设施对UTC连续性的需求是推动此次决定的重要因素。

**技术细节**

闰秒的引入或移除会打断计算机系统内部的时间流，给依赖精确时间同步的应用带来巨大困难。例如，大型科技公司如Meta、AWS和Google已投入巨资开发“时间平滑”技术来应对正闰秒，但仍面临挑战。闰秒的决定过程依赖于委员会，无法自动化，增加了系统更新的复杂性和风险。相比之下，时间区域和夏令时等时间偏移更容易管理，而绝对时间尺度的突然变化对计算机系统而言是难以处理的。

**实用价值**

UTC不再引入闰秒，将极大地简化全球时间同步的复杂性，降低因闰秒处理不当而引发的系统故障风险，尤其对交通、能源、通信和航天等关键基础设施意义重大。虽然UTC与地球自转的脱钩可能在遥远的未来产生可观的偏差，但这一问题将由未来的技术和标准来解决。此举消除了一个长期存在的技术痛点，使技术人员能够更专注于其他紧迫的时间相关问题，如Y2K38问题。
